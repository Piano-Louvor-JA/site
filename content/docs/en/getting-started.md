# Your first worship service with PIANO LouvorJA

Get everything ready before the service: open PIANO, search for and download a hymn, put together a simple liturgy and have the projection ready. During the program, you only choose the next item and advance the slides.

## What should I prepare beforehand?

Use a computer with PIANO LouvorJA open. To project, connect a second screen, projector or TV. Download the hymns for the first time with an internet connection; anything you download will remain available even without a connection.

1. Turn on the computer, TV or projector.
2. Open PIANO LouvorJA.
3. Confirm that you can see the operator screen, where you prepare the service.
4. If the interface looks small, use the zoom buttons in the header.
5. Keep the internet connected while you choose and download the content you need.

On first launch, the catalog of hymns, songbooks and cover art can be prepared in the background. You can keep using the application while this happens.

## How do I find hymn No. 5 or search by title?

The Media Center brings together hymns, songbooks, lyrics, slides and audio. You can locate a hymn by number, by title or by part of the lyrics.

1. In the navigation bar, open **Media Center**.
2. Click the search field.
3. To find it by number, type `5`.
4. To find it by title, type the name or a few words of the hymn.
5. Choose the result matching the hymn from the Adventist Hymnal or the songbook that will be used.
6. Open the hymn to check the title, lyrics and cover art before moving on.

If there is more than one similar result, check the number and title before adding the hymn to the program. This avoids projecting a different song during the praise time.

## How do I download the hymn to use in the service?

Before the service, download everything you might use. The progress indicator shows the status; if the application closes or the connection fails, the progress is saved to continue later.

1. With the hymn open, locate the **download** button.
2. Click to start the download.
3. Wait for the progress bar to finish.
4. Repeat for each hymn, songbook or media item in the program.
5. Before the service, open the downloaded items to confirm they are available.

A downloaded hymn stays available on the device without internet. Settings, liturgies and download progress also remain saved on the device.

## How do I build a simple liturgy?

The Liturgy organizes the service in the order it will happen. For a simple program, create a praise block, add the hymns and, if you wish, include a Bible, video, audio or note item for the team.

1. Open **Liturgy**.
2. Create a new liturgy.
3. Add a **Category/Separator** with a simple name, such as `Praise`.
4. Enter start and end times if your team uses time-based planning.
5. Add an item of type **Music**.
6. Search for and select the first hymn you just checked.
7. Add the other hymns in the order of the service.
8. If needed, add another category, such as `Word` or `Sabbath School`.
9. Include **Bible**, **Video**, **Audio**, **Images**, **PDF**, **PowerPoint presentation**, **Website/Online video** or **Notes** items according to the program.
10. Drag the items into the correct order.
11. Save the liturgy.

A category groups the items inside it. When you drag it, the grouped items move along with it. Notes are for the team only: they are not shown to the congregation.

## How do I set up the church screen?

The operator screen is for the person running the application. The church screen shows only the projection: lyrics, Bible verses, clock, countdown and other chosen content.

1. Connect the projector, monitor or TV to the computer.
2. Open the hymn in the **Media Center**.
3. Click **Project**.
4. Check on the second screen that the lyrics appear.
5. Open **Settings** if you need to adjust the background color, text color, font size, alignment, background image or text box.
6. For a light background, prefer a dark text box; for a dark background, a light box or no box may work better.
7. Return to the hymn and have the first slide ready before the praise time starts.

With no lyrics selected, the projection can show the album cover with the title. This is useful while the congregation prepares to sing.

## How do I lead the service and advance the slides?

During the service, open the liturgy and follow the prepared order. Music, Bible and utility items can be projected according to each moment of the program.

1. Open the saved liturgy.
2. Select the first music item.
3. Click **Project** if the projection is not yet active.
4. Advance through the verse, line or chorus slides as the congregation sings.
5. Use the progress bar and the next-phrase preview to keep track of where you are.
6. When finished, mark the item as done and open the next one.
7. To project a Scripture message, open **Bible**, choose the version, book, chapter and verse, then click **Project**.
8. To end the projection, press `ESC` and confirm the action.

Audio keeps playing while you navigate to another section. This way, you can let the hymn continue and prepare the next verse or liturgy item. When you switch hymns while audio is playing, the application makes a smooth transition.

## What do I do if the internet goes down during the service?

Keep going with the hymns and media you have already downloaded. Local content, liturgies, settings and saved progress do not depend on the internet once prepared.

1. Do not close the liturgy in use.
2. Use only hymns and media you confirmed before the service.
3. If you need to search for something new, wait for the connection to return.
4. At the end of the service, check pending downloads for the next program.

## How do I keep a copy of my preparation?

The application saves your work on the device itself. To switch computers or keep a backup copy, use the backup feature.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Look for the **Backup** option.
3. Export the settings and data.
4. Keep the file in a safe place before formatting or replacing the computer.
