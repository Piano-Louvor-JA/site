# Common problems and solutions

Start from the symptom, test the solution before the service and keep an alternative ready for the projection. What has already been downloaded, your liturgy and the local settings remain saved on the device.

## What should I check before asking for help?

Run this check before sending a message. It resolves the most frequent errors and gives the team the information they need if the problem continues.

1. Close and reopen the application or reload the page.
2. Confirm that the computer, projector, TV and audio output are on.
3. Check that the video cable is connected and the TV is on the correct input.
4. Check the internet if you are trying to download content or using the web version.
5. Open a hymn that is already downloaded to separate an internet failure from an app failure.
6. Test the projection with a hymn and a verse.
7. Note the system, browser or application version, the affected module and the message displayed.
8. Take a screenshot without showing passwords, personal data or church information.

## The application does not open or does not load. What do I do?

Reload first. In the web version, the first access depends on the internet; in the application, the **Try again** option can recover a temporary failure.

1. Reload the page.
2. If you are in the browser, use `Ctrl+F5` to refresh the cache.
3. Check that the internet works on another site.
4. Test in an incognito window to find out whether an extension is interfering.
5. Clear the site data in the browser settings and open it again.
6. If an error screen appears, click **Try again**.
7. If the app reports a missing bridge in browser mode, continue with the available local data or use the version appropriate for your device.

If it persists, send the checklist data and report at which moment the error appeared.

## The hymn catalog does not load. What do I do?

Check the connection. The application tries catalog sources in a cascade; even so, items that were already downloaded remain available offline.

1. Check the internet.
2. Reload the application.
3. Open an already-downloaded hymn to continue the rehearsal or service, if needed.
4. Do not delete local data during a program.
5. When the connection returns, try the search again.

## The hymn does not download. What do I do?

Watch the progress bar and keep the connection active. Closing the application should not erase the progress: it is saved to continue later.

1. Check that the internet is stable.
2. Start the download and wait a few moments.
3. If the progress stops, reload or reopen the application.
4. Search for the same hymn again and check whether the download resumed.
5. For the service, use only hymns whose download you confirmed beforehand.

## The audio does not play. What do I do?

Start by checking the computer's volume and sound output. PIANO keeps the audio playing even when you navigate to other sections, so the problem may be in the selected output or in the item itself.

1. Check that the player is playing, not paused.
2. Increase the volume in PIANO and on the computer.
3. Check that the correct sound mixer, speaker or headset is selected and powered on.
4. Test another hymn with audio.
5. If the problem is with external video or audio on the desktop, open **Settings** and review **External player**.
6. Choose **System-associated** to use the default player or select another detected player.
7. Test again before starting the service.

## The video, PDF or presentation opens in the wrong program. What do I do?

On the desktop, PIANO can use the system-associated player or another detected/configured player.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Go to **External player**.
3. Choose **System-associated** to use the computer's default program.
4. If needed, select a detected player, such as VLC, or add another executable through the file picker.
5. Open the item again to test.
6. To go back, remove the custom player and use the system-associated one.

## The projection does not appear. What do I do?

Confirm the cable, the TV/projector input and that the projection of the correct module is active. The church screen is separate from the operator screen.

1. Turn on the projector or TV.
2. Choose the correct input on the TV or projector.
3. Check the video cable on the computer and on the screen.
4. Open a hymn in the **Media Center**.
5. Click **Project**.
6. Advance one slide to test the update.
7. If nothing appears, reopen the projection window in the right module, such as **Media** or **Bible**.
8. Test with other content, such as a verse or the clock.

## The second screen is not detected. What do I do?

Recognition of an extra screen depends on the computer, the cable and the operating system. First confirm the physical connection and the computer's own display settings.

1. Turn the TV or projector off and on again.
2. Unplug and reconnect the cable.
3. Test another cable or another input, if available.
4. Open the computer's display settings and confirm that the second screen appears.
5. Choose the dual-screen mode appropriate for your computer.
6. Once the system recognizes the screen, return to PIANO and test **Project**.

On Windows, the `Win+P` shortcut toggles between "Duplicate" and "Extend" displays. On macOS, open System Settings → Displays and drag the screens to the correct physical positions. On Linux, monitor settings are under Settings → Display (the name varies by distribution).

## The lyrics are misaligned or hard to read. What do I do?

Adjust the projection settings. Lyrics and Bible have their own visual options in the application.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **Projection**, adjust the background color, text color, font size and weight.
3. Adjust the horizontal and vertical alignment.
4. Enable the text box and change opacity/border if the background hurts readability.
5. Replace or adjust the background image if necessary.
6. For the Bible, also review the specific Bible style settings.
7. Test from far away, at the position where the congregation will see the screen.

## The TV or Stage does not connect. What do I do?

Confirm the network and the pairing. The manual describes P2P connection on the same network or via server, in separate tabs.

1. Check that the TV/Stage receiver and the control device are powered on.
2. For P2P, connect both to the same local network.
3. Avoid guest Wi‑Fi that isolates connected devices.
4. Reopen the pairing screen on the control and on the receiver.
5. Reconnect and test a hymn and a verse.
6. If the connection fails during the service, use the cable-connected second screen as an alternative, when available.

If the TV shows old or frozen content after reconnecting, close and reopen the Stage receiver on the TV (or reload the page, in the browser) before trying to reconnect from the PIANO side. The TV may keep in memory the screen of a previous connection that has already ended.

## The update does not seem to have arrived. What do I do?

In the web version, updates are applied by reloading the page. Update before the service, not minutes before starting.

1. Reload the page.
2. Use `Ctrl+F5` if you need to refresh the cache.
3. Close and reopen the application when using the installed version.
4. Check that the essential features keep working: search, downloaded hymn, audio, liturgy and projection.
5. If a change prevents use, record the version, browser and symptom for support.

## Can I lose my liturgies, downloads or settings?

Closing the application, restarting the computer or losing the internet should not erase the local data already saved. Even so, make a backup before switching machines or performing system maintenance.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Use **Backup** to export data and settings.
3. Keep the copy outside the computer that will be changed.
4. Test the import on the other computer before relying on it during a service.
5. Do not remove application files or data without having a copy.
