## How does the application organize the service?

PIANO separates the operator screen from the church screen. On the operator screen, the team searches hymns, organizes the liturgy and controls features. On the projection, the congregation sees lyrics, Bible verses, clock, timer, countdown or draws.

1. Prepare downloads, liturgy and appearance before the service.
2. Control from the computer or a paired device.
3. Project to another monitor, projector, TV or Stage receiver.
4. Advance the content according to each moment of the program.
5. Save a backup before switching computers.

## What does PIANO save on the device?

Downloads, progress, liturgies and settings are saved locally. Therefore, you can close the application, restart the computer or lose the internet without losing the preparation already done.

To protect this work when switching computers, use **Settings** and export a **Backup**. Keep the copy before any formatting or maintenance.

## How do I keep the projection readable?

Adjust the projection before the congregation arrives. In **Settings**, choose the background color, text color, font size and weight, alignment, text box and background image. The Bible has specific style settings.

1. Project a hymn with the room's real lighting.
2. Read the screen from a distance.
3. Use a dark box over light backgrounds.
4. Increase the font if reading becomes difficult.
5. Also test a Bible verse and the clock.
