# Use PIANO LouvorJA on Samsung TV

The Samsung TV should receive the projection through the **Stage**: another device controls the service and the TV shows only the church screen. On the download page, the Samsung TV channel appears when a file has been published; with no file available, use the TV's browser to access the web app or a compatible receiver connected to the TV.

## What is the Stage?

The Stage is the receiver that runs on the TV and shows the projection sent by PIANO. The team keeps operating from a computer, phone or other device; the TV does not need to display the searches, the liturgy or the control buttons.

With the Stage connected, the congregation can follow:

- lyrics of hymns and songbooks;
- Bible verses;
- clock, timer and countdown;
- other projections started by the operator.

## What should I prepare beforehand?

Prepare the network and the devices before Sabbath or the program.

1. Connect the Samsung TV to the church network.
2. Connect the operator computer or phone to the same network when using local pairing.
3. Open [download](/download) on another device and check whether there is an active download for **Samsung/Tizen**.
4. If an official file exists, use only that file and the instructions on the download page.
5. If there is no file, open the TV's browser or connect a compatible receiver to the HDMI input.
6. Run the test with the TV on and the sound adjusted.

The download page shows cards for Samsung/Tizen, but the file depends on a version being published for the receiver. This documentation does not mention any specific Samsung store.

## How do I use the TV's browser as an alternative?

Use the browser only as an alternative when no receiver is installed. Navigation and compatibility depend on the TV model.

1. On the remote control, press **Home**.
2. Open **Apps**, **Internet** or the browser available on the TV.
3. Type `app.pianolouvorja.com.br`.
4. Wait for the app to load.
5. Run a readability and navigation test.
6. If the TV's browser does not work well, use a computer or TV Box with the Stage connected to the HDMI input.

Do not try to install an Android APK on the Samsung TV: Tizen is not Android.

## How do I pair the TV with PIANO?

Pairing links the TV's receiver to the device that controls the program.

1. Open the Stage or the web app on the device that will be connected to the TV.
2. Open PIANO on the operator computer or phone.
3. Go to the **Remote control and Stage** area.
4. Choose the pairing method shown: same network or via server.
5. Confirm the data or code presented on both devices.
6. Wait for the connection confirmation.
7. Project a test hymn.
8. Advance one verse and confirm that the TV follows along.

All devices must be on the same network for local pairing. If the church uses separate networks for visitors and equipment, connect the operator and the receiver to the equipment network.

## How do I test the complete projection?

Test before the congregation arrives.

1. In the **Media Center**, open a sacred hymn and click **Project**.
2. Advance through verses and chorus.
3. In **Bible**, project a short passage.
4. In **Utilities**, test the clock and countdown.
5. In **Settings**, increase the font size and adjust contrast, if needed.
6. Confirm that the TV does not show control menus.
7. End the projection with **ESC** on the operator device and confirm that the TV clears the screen.

## How do I improve readability on the TV?

The adjustment is made on the operator device and applies to what the congregation sees.

1. Open **Settings** in PIANO.
2. Go to **Projection**.
3. Choose a background and text with contrast.
4. Increase the font size for reading at a distance.
5. Enable the text box over light images.
6. Adjust the horizontal and vertical alignment.
7. Redo the test from the farthest point in the hall.

## What if the TV does not appear in the pairing?

1. Confirm that the TV/receiver and the operator are on the same network.
2. Close and reopen the Stage or the TV's browser.
3. Check that the TV is not on an isolated guest network.
4. Restart only the receiver or the TV, then try pairing again.
5. If it still does not work, use an HDMI cable temporarily for the projection and do not interrupt the service.

## How do I organize the program on the operator device?

The TV receives the projection; the preparation should happen on the computer, phone or tablet used by the team.

1. Open **Liturgy** on the operator device.
2. Create categories for the moments of the service.
3. Add songs, Bible verses, files and notes.
4. Reorder the items to reflect the actual program.
5. Download beforehand the hymns and content needed.
6. Save and review with whoever will lead the program.
7. Have the first item prepared before pairing the TV.

## How do I avoid interruptions on the Samsung TV?

1. Keep the TV plugged into the outlet, not just in standby mode.
2. Disable automatic shutoff timers when they are active.
3. Confirm the Wi-Fi network before the program.
4. Keep the remote control with good batteries.
5. Do not change network settings during the service.
6. Avoid opening extra applications on the TV.
7. Keep an HDMI cable available as a contingency plan.

## How do I use the clock and timers on the Stage?

1. On the operator device, open **Utilities**.
2. Choose **Clock**, **Timer** or **Countdown**.
3. Adjust the clock's color and format when needed.
4. Start the timer or countdown at the set time.
5. Check on the TV that the display is clear.
6. Save marks on the timer when the team needs to record moments.
7. End the projection when moving to the next liturgy item.

## How do I handle a last-minute change?

1. Keep the operator on the main device.
2. Use the **Media Center** search to locate the needed hymn.
3. Project and advance normally; the TV will receive the update.
4. Record significant changes in the liturgy notes.
5. Avoid restarting the TV while the projection is still working.
6. If the receiver fails, switch temporarily to HDMI.
7. Redo the pairing after the service, calmly.

## Final checklist before the service

1. Confirm that PIANO opens on the chosen device.
2. Confirm that hymns and materials are available.
3. Confirm that the liturgy is in the correct order.
4. Confirm that the church screen receives a test projection.
5. Confirm text size and contrast against the hall's background.
6. Confirm network, power and the necessary cables.
7. Leave one person in charge of the operation.
