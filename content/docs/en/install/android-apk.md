# Install PIANO LouvorJA via APK on Android

Download the APK only from the official PIANO LouvorJA page. Android will ask for permission for the browser to install this file; allow only the browser used for the download, complete the installation and open the app to prepare the content for the service.

## Where do I download the official APK?

The Android APK is published in the official releases and the download page shows the correct path.

1. Open the browser on your phone or tablet.
2. Go to [download](/download).
3. Find the **Android** section.
4. Tap the button to download the APK.
5. Wait for the download to finish completely.
6. Open the download notification or the **Downloads** folder.

Do not install an APK received through groups, e-mail or a pendrive without confirming its origin. The official page is the reference for the current version.

## How do I allow the APK installation?

Menu names vary by manufacturer, but the flow is similar: Android asks for authorization for the app that opened the file, usually Chrome or the file manager.

1. Tap the downloaded APK file.
2. When the security warning appears, tap **Settings**.
3. Enable **Allow from this source** or **Install unknown apps**.
4. Go back to the previous screen.
5. Tap **Install**.
6. Wait for it to finish.
7. Tap **Open**.

After the installation, you can disable that permission in the same menu. This prevents other files from being installed without your confirmation.

## Where do I find this permission if the warning does not appear?

Look for the equivalent name in the device's system.

1. Open the Android **Settings**.
2. Go to **Apps**.
3. Choose the browser used to download the APK, such as Chrome, or the file manager.
4. Open **Install unknown apps**.
5. Enable **Allow from this source**.
6. Return to the **Downloads** folder and open the APK again.

On some devices, the option is under **Security** or **Privacy**. What matters is allowing only the app that will perform the installation, not an unchecked general permission.

## What should I do on first use?

Prepare the content before the church program.

1. Open **PIANO LouvorJA**.
2. Keep the device connected to the internet during the initial preparation.
3. Adjust the interface size with the **−** and **＋** buttons, if they appear in the header.
4. Open **Media Center** and search for the sacred hymns of the program.
5. Download the necessary content; progress is saved.
6. In **Liturgy**, organize songs, Bible, files and team notes.
7. In **Settings**, test the projection colors and text size.

Downloaded content and settings stay on the device. For a service without internet, download the items in advance and test beforehand.

## How do I use the phone as a remote control?

PIANO can pair devices over the same network or through a server.

1. Open the **Remote control** area in PIANO.
2. Choose the available pairing tab: same network or server.
3. Follow the code or confirmation shown on the screen.
4. Test searching for a hymn, advancing a lyric and switching a Bible verse.
5. Also test clearing the selection and ending a projection before the service.

The remote control can help the team advance lyrics, Bible verses, timer, countdown and clock without standing next to the computer.

## Can I use it on an Android TV Box?

Yes, as an option for compatible Android devices. Download the APK from the official page in the TV Box's browser or transfer it from a verified official source. The best use for a TV is the **Stage**, which receives the projection controlled by another device.

1. Connect the TV Box to the same network as the device controlling PIANO.
2. Open the TV Box's browser and go to [download](/download), if it is usable.
3. Download the official APK and authorize the installation for that browser or file manager.
4. Open the installed app.
5. On the operator device, open the **Stage** pairing.
6. Connect the TV receiver to the network and complete the pairing shown by PIANO.
7. Project a test hymn and confirm that the TV shows only the church screen.

For Android TV and Google TV specific steps, see the dedicated page.

## How do I avoid problems on the day of the service?

1. Keep the device charged or plugged in.
2. Download hymns and media before the program.
3. Connect all devices to the same network when using the Stage or local remote control.
4. Test the entire liturgy, including videos, PDFs and Bible passages.
5. Do not update the Android system or switch apps minutes before starting.

## How do I project lyrics and Bible after pairing?

1. On the operator device, open the **Media Center**.
2. Search by title or by a section of the hymn.
3. Open the result and tap **Project**.
4. Advance verses and chorus; the Stage follows on the TV.
5. Open the **Bible**, choose the version, book, chapter and verses.
6. Tap **Project** to send the passage.
7. Use **ESC** or the available command to end the projection.

## How do I prepare the liturgy on Android?

1. Open **Liturgy** and create a program.
2. Add separators for each part of the service.
3. Add songs, files, links and notes.
4. Use complementary titles, such as **Prayer** or **Welcome**, when needed.
5. Drag the items into the final order.
6. Save and check the result.
7. During the program, advance item by item.

## How do I take care of the battery and the network?

1. Charge the phone, tablet or TV Box before use.
2. Keep the operator device plugged in during long programs.
3. Connect the equipment to the same network for the local Stage.
4. Avoid switching between Wi-Fi networks during the service.
5. Disable notifications that could distract the operator.
6. Have hymns and media downloaded before starting.
7. Test the connection and the projection in advance.

## What should I do if Android blocks the installation?

1. Check that the file came from the official page.
2. Check that the download completed.
3. Allow **Install unknown apps** only for the app that opened the APK.
4. Return to the file and tap **Install** again.
5. If storage is full, free up space and try again.
6. If the APK is not compatible with the device, use the PWA in the browser.
7. Do not disable system protections beyond the one-time installation permission.

## Final checklist before the service

1. Confirm that PIANO opens on the chosen device.
2. Confirm that hymns and materials are available.
3. Confirm that the liturgy is in the correct order.
4. Confirm that the church screen receives a test projection.
5. Confirm text size and contrast against the hall's background.
6. Confirm network, power and the necessary cables.
7. Leave one person in charge of the operation.
