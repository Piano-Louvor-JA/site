# Use PIANO LouvorJA on LG webOS TV

The LG webOS TV should receive the projection through the **Stage**, while a computer or phone controls the service. Check the download page to see whether an official webOS file has been published; if not, use the TV's browser as an alternative or connect a compatible receiver via HDMI.

## How does the Stage work on the LG TV?

The Stage turns the TV into the congregation's screen. The operator prepares and advances the program on another device, and the LG shows only the projected content.

This makes it possible to follow:

- lyrics from the Adventist Hymnal, albums and songbooks;
- verses selected in the Bible;
- clock and counters for the start and progress of the service;
- projections started in any PIANO module.

## What do I need before pairing?

1. Turn on the LG TV and connect it to the church network.
2. Connect the operator computer or phone to the same network for local pairing.
3. Open [download](/download) on another device.
4. Check the TVs section and the **LG/webOS** card.
5. Use the official file only when the download button is active.
6. If no file has been published, prepare the webOS browser or an external receiver via HDMI.
7. Test the TV's network connection before the program.

The download page recognizes the webOS channel when an asset has been published by the receiver. There is no confirmation, in the sources consulted, of a current publication on the LG Content Store.

## How do I use the webOS browser as an alternative?

The browser can be useful when no receiver app is available. Names may change depending on the webOS version.

1. Press the **Home** button on the remote control.
2. Open **Web Browser**, **Browser** or the equivalent option.
3. Type `app.pianolouvorja.com.br`.
4. Wait for the page to load.
5. Run an opening and readability test.
6. If there are freezes or difficult navigation, use the Stage on an external device connected via HDMI.

The browser is an alternative; the recommended flow for dedicated projection remains keeping the control on another device and the TV as the Stage screen.

## How do I pair the Stage with the operator device?

1. Open the Stage on the receiver connected to the TV or open the web app prepared on the TV.
2. Open PIANO on the computer, phone or tablet that will control the service.
3. Go to **Remote control and Stage**.
4. Choose pairing on the same network or via server, according to the available screen.
5. Confirm the code or requested data.
6. Wait for the connection status to appear.
7. Open a hymn and click **Project**.
8. Advance one verse to confirm the update on the TV.

Use the same network for both devices when choosing local mode. A guest network can prevent them from seeing each other.

## How do I run the service test?

1. Project a hymn and advance lyric by lyric.
2. Test a passage from the **Bible**.
3. Open **Utilities** and project the clock.
4. Test the timer and countdown if they will be part of the liturgy.
5. Open the planned liturgy and check the media items.
6. Check the TV volume when there is audio.
7. End a projection and confirm that the TV reacts correctly.

Prepare media and hymns beforehand. PIANO saves downloads, progress and settings on the device that is controlling the program.

## How do I adjust font, background and contrast?

1. On the operator device, open **Settings**.
2. Go to **Projection**.
3. Set the background color and text color.
4. Increase the font for the hall's distance.
5. Enable the text box over light backgrounds.
6. Adjust the alignment and background image.
7. Ask someone to check readability from the back of the church.

## What should I do if the LG TV does not connect?

1. Confirm the Wi-Fi or network cable on both the TV and the operator device.
2. Close and reopen the browser or the TV receiver.
3. Check that the operator device is not on a guest network.
4. Redo the pairing using the via-server option, if it is available in PIANO.
5. If urgent, connect the computer to the TV via HDMI and continue the service.

## How do I organize the service on the operator device?

1. Open **Liturgy** on the computer, phone or tablet that will control the TV.
2. Create categories for each moment of the program.
3. Add songs, media files, links and team notes.
4. Drag items to adjust the sequence.
5. Download beforehand the hymns and materials needed.
6. Save the program.
7. Have the first item open before starting.

## How do I use the Bible and utilities on the LG TV?

1. On the operator device, open **Bible**.
2. Choose the version, book, chapter and verses.
3. Click **Project** and check the passage on the TV.
4. Open **Utilities** to display the clock or timers.
5. Adjust the colors to match the TV's readability.
6. Start the timer or countdown.
7. Test ending the projection before the service.

## How do I avoid problems before the program?

1. Turn on the TV in advance.
2. Check the internet or local network.
3. Keep the remote control nearby.
4. Run the test from the back of the church.
5. Check the HDMI cables if there is an external receiver.
6. Do not update the TV minutes before the service.
7. Keep an HDMI alternative ready in case of a network failure.

## How do I handle a program change?

1. The operator searches for the new hymn in the **Media Center**.
2. Projects the item directly to the TV.
3. Updates the liturgy if the team needs to record the change.
4. Uses notes to guide the next person in the program.
5. Keeps the TV on the Stage, without trying to control the service with the TV remote.
6. Visually confirms the lyric before asking the congregation to sing.
7. Resumes the normal sequence when the change is over.

## Final checklist before the service

1. Confirm that PIANO opens on the chosen device.
2. Confirm that hymns and materials are available.
3. Confirm that the liturgy is in the correct order.
4. Confirm that the church screen receives a test projection.
5. Confirm text size and contrast against the hall's background.
6. Confirm network, power and the necessary cables.
7. Leave one person in charge of the operation.
