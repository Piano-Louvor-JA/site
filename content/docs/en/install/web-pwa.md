# Use PIANO LouvorJA in the browser and as an app

Open PIANO directly at [app.pianolouvorja.com.br](https://app.pianolouvorja.com.br). In Chrome, Edge or Android, you can add it as an app to the home screen; after the first load, it can remain available offline according to the content already stored on the device.

## How do I open PIANO without installing anything?

The browser is the fastest way to get started — useful for trying PIANO, using another computer or supporting a program outside the church.

1. Open an up-to-date browser.
2. Type `app.pianolouvorja.com.br`.
3. Wait for the application to load.
4. Open **Media Center** to find hymns and songbooks.
5. Use **Liturgy** to organize the program.
6. Test the projection before starting the service.

For a better multi-screen projection experience, the computer is usually the most practical choice. The browser remains useful on tablets, phones and shared devices.

## How do I install as an app in Chrome or Edge on the computer?

The PWA installation creates a shortcut that opens PIANO in its own window, without having to look for the browser tab.

1. Open [app.pianolouvorja.com.br](https://app.pianolouvorja.com.br) in Chrome or Edge.
2. Look for the install icon next to the site address, when it appears.
3. Click **Install** or **Install PIANO LouvorJA**.
4. Confirm the installation.
5. Open PIANO from the shortcut created in the applications menu or on the Desktop.
6. Run a test of hymn, Bible and projection.

If the icon does not appear, open the browser's three-dot menu and look for **Install app**, **Install this page as an app** or a similar option.

## How do I add it to the home screen on Android?

On Android, PIANO can be available as an icon on the home screen.

1. Open [app.pianolouvorja.com.br](https://app.pianolouvorja.com.br) in Chrome.
2. Tap the three-dot menu.
3. Choose **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
4. Confirm the name and tap **Add** or **Install**.
5. Locate the PIANO icon on the home screen or in the app list.
6. Open it from the icon and test before the service.

In other browsers, the wording may vary. Look for the option that adds the site as an app or to the home screen.

## Does PIANO work offline in the browser?

After the first load, the PWA can remain available offline according to the data and content already stored on the device. Prepare everything with internet before the service.

1. With internet, open PIANO at least once.
2. Search for and download in advance the hymns that will be used.
3. Open the liturgy and confirm that the needed items are ready.
4. Test the projection while the internet is still available.
5. Afterwards, if the connection drops, use what has already been prepared and saved on the device.

Do not wait until the program starts to rely on content not yet loaded. The initial access and new updates require a connection.

## When should I use the browser, the PWA or the installed application?

Choose according to how the church will use it.

| Situation                                                 | Best choice                                    |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Quick test or borrowed computer                           | Browser at `app.pianolouvorja.com.br`          |
| Always on the same computer without a traditional install | PWA installed from Chrome or Edge              |
| Two-monitor projection with the church's fixed routine    | Application for Windows, macOS or Linux        |
| Control from a phone or tablet                            | PWA in the browser or added to the home screen |
| Network-connected TV to receive the projection            | Stage on a compatible device                   |

The installed version is preferable when the team needs a stable operation, with the operator screen separated from the church screen.

## How do I prepare a program in the PWA?

The service organization is the same: prepare beforehand and advance during the program.

1. In **Media Center**, search for hymns by name or part of the lyrics.
2. Download the hymns to make the content available on the device.
3. In **Liturgy**, create categories such as **Welcome**, **Sabbath School**, **Praise**, **Message** and **Closing**.
4. Add songs, videos, images, PDF, presentation, links and team notes.
5. Drag the items to adjust the order.
6. Save and reopen to double-check.
7. During the service, advance item by item and track completed items.

## How do I make the church screen readable?

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Go to **Projection**.
3. Choose colors with strong contrast.
4. Adjust the font between 50% and 200% according to the distance of the congregation.
5. Enable the text box if the background has a light image.
6. Adjust alignment and background.
7. Test on the projector or TV before starting.

## How do I use the Bible and utilities in the PWA?

1. Open **Bible**.
2. Choose the version, book, chapter and verse or verses.
3. Click **Project** to send the text to the church screen.
4. For the time, open **Utilities** and choose **Clock**.
5. Adjust the color, 12- or 24-hour format and seconds.
6. To control time, use **Timer** or **Countdown**.
7. Test everything before the program starts.

## How do I control PIANO remotely?

1. Open the **Remote control and Stage** area on the main device.
2. Choose pairing on the same network or via server.
3. Complete the confirmation shown on the devices.
4. Test searching for a hymn and opening a song.
5. Test advancing Bible verses and clearing the selection.
6. Test starting, pausing and resetting timers.
7. Confirm that the second screen does not show the team's controls.

## What should I do if the PWA does not open offline?

1. Connect the device to the internet.
2. Open `app.pianolouvorja.com.br` in the browser.
3. Wait for the page to finish loading.
4. Do the preparation and downloads again.
5. Keep the hymns and liturgy saved before disconnecting.
6. Refresh the page when there is internet to receive the current version.
7. For a fixed projection routine, consider the desktop application.

## How do I keep the browser ready for the program?

1. Close tabs that will not be used.
2. Disable browser notifications, if possible.
3. Use fullscreen only if it does not hide needed controls.
4. Check that the computer will not go to sleep.
5. Keep the charger and network connection available.
6. Open the liturgy and have the first item ready.
7. Run a final projection test.

## Final checklist before the service

1. Confirm that PIANO opens on the chosen device.
2. Confirm that hymns and materials are available.
3. Confirm that the liturgy is in the correct order.
4. Confirm that the church screen receives a test projection.
5. Confirm text size and contrast against the hall's background.
6. Confirm network, power and the necessary cables.
7. Leave one person in charge of the operation.
