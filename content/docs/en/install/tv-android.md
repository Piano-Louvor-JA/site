# Use PIANO LouvorJA on Android TV and Google TV

On Android TV or Google TV, install the official APK through the TV's browser or by safe transfer and use the **Stage** to receive the projection. Another device controls hymns, Bible, liturgy and utilities; the TV is dedicated to what the congregation sees.

## What does the Stage do on Android TV?

The Stage receives the PIANO projection over the network. It separates the team's work from the congregation's screen.

On the TV, the congregation follows only:

- lyrics of Adventist hymns and songbooks;
- Bible verses;
- clock, timer and countdown;
- other projections opened by the operator.

On the computer, phone or tablet, the team keeps the searches, liturgy, settings and controls.

## How do I prepare the TV before installation?

1. Turn on the Android TV or Google TV.
2. Connect it to the church network.
3. Check that the operator computer or phone can join the same network.
4. Open [download](/download) on a trusted device.
5. Check whether there is an official file for **Android TV** or a compatible APK.
6. Download only through the official channel.
7. Reserve time to test before the program.

The download page recognizes Android TV as a receiver platform when an asset has been published. The Android APK is also an official channel for installation on compatible Android devices.

## How do I download the APK through the TV's browser?

Use this path when the TV has a working browser.

1. Open the browser on the Android TV or Google TV.
2. Go to [download](/download).
3. Locate the official PIANO APK.
4. Download the file and wait for it to finish.
5. Open the downloads list or the file manager.
6. If the TV asks for permission, allow installing unknown apps only for the browser or manager used.
7. Choose **Install**.
8. Open PIANO or the installed receiver.

Menu names may change by brand. Look for **Security**, **Apps**, **Permissions** or **Install unknown apps**.

## How do I install by safe transfer?

Use this path if the TV's browser is limited. Transfer only the APK downloaded from the official page.

1. Download the official APK on a trusted computer or phone.
2. Transfer the file to a pendrive or to a file method available on the TV.
3. Connect the pendrive to the TV, if that is the chosen method.
4. Open the TV's file manager.
5. If needed, authorize that manager to install unknown apps.
6. Open the APK and choose **Install**.
7. After the installation, remove the pendrive to avoid confusion with old files.

Do not use an APK of unknown origin. Keep a verified copy of the official file in case the church's internet is unstable.

## How do I pair it with PIANO?

1. Open the Stage or receiver on the Android TV.
2. Open PIANO on the team's computer, phone or tablet.
3. Go to **Remote control and Stage**.
4. Choose pairing on the same network or via server, according to the displayed option.
5. Confirm the code or data requested by both devices.
6. Wait for the connection to be confirmed.
7. In the **Media Center**, open a hymn and click **Project**.
8. Advance one verse and check the TV screen.

For local pairing, both devices must be on the same network. Guest networks usually isolate devices from each other.

## How do I test before the service?

Run a complete test; do not test only the installation.

1. Project a hymn and advance through verses, chorus and cover.
2. Project a Bible passage.
3. Test clock, timer and countdown in **Utilities**.
4. Open a liturgy with the categories of the program.
5. Check the videos, audios, images, PDFs or presentations used in the service.
6. Adjust font and contrast in **Settings**.
7. Check readability from the farthest point in the hall.
8. End the projection and confirm that the TV reacts.

## How do I make the projection readable?

1. On the operator device, open **Settings**.
2. Go to **Projection**.
3. Use high contrast between text and background.
4. Adjust the font size for the church's distance.
5. Enable the text box when there is a light image in the background.
6. Adjust the horizontal and vertical alignment.
7. Test a long lyric and a Bible passage.

## What should I do if the APK does not install or the Stage does not connect?

1. Check that the file came from the official page.
2. Check that the TV has free space and that the download completed.
3. Confirm the installation authorization for the browser or file manager used.
4. Restart the receiver or the TV and try again.
5. Confirm that the operator and the TV are on the same network.
6. If the connection fails close to the service, temporarily use a computer connected to the TV via HDMI.

## How do I organize the liturgy on the operator device?

1. Open **Liturgy** on the team's computer, phone or tablet.
2. Create categories to separate each moment of the service.
3. Add songs, Bible, local media, links and notes.
4. Drag the items into the final order.
5. Download hymns and needed content in advance.
6. Save and review the program.
7. During the service, advance item by item while the TV receives the projection.

## How do I use the remote control with Android TV?

1. Pair the control device through the PIANO menu.
2. Test opening a song with cover and lyrics.
3. Test advancing and clearing verses.
4. Test closing the projection.
5. Test starting, pausing and resetting timers.
6. Test the clock and countdown.
7. Leave only one person in charge of the controls during the service.

## How do I avoid power, network and image problems?

1. Keep the TV and the operator device plugged in.
2. Check the network before opening the Stage.
3. Disable automatic sleep, if the TV offers that option.
4. Keep the remote control charged or with good batteries.
5. Adjust the TV's brightness and picture mode for the hall's lighting.
6. Do not change Wi-Fi networks during the program.
7. Keep an HDMI cable ready as a contingency.

## How do I handle a last-minute change?

1. On the operator device, use the **Media Center** search.
2. Open the needed hymn or content.
3. Click **Project**.
4. Check on the TV before moving on.
5. Use the liturgy notes to guide the team.
6. Resume the sequence when the change is over.
7. Avoid reinstalling or updating applications during the service.

## Final checklist before the service

1. Confirm that PIANO opens on the chosen device.
2. Confirm that hymns and materials are available.
3. Confirm that the liturgy is in the correct order.
4. Confirm that the church screen receives a test projection.
5. Confirm text size and contrast against the hall's background.
6. Confirm network, power and the necessary cables.
7. Leave one person in charge of the operation.
