# Install PIANO LouvorJA on macOS

Download the DMG file that matches your Mac, drag PIANO into **Applications** and open it. Macs with an M1 chip or newer use ARM64; Intel Macs use X64. If macOS blocks the first launch, open it through the right-click menu.

## How do I know if my Mac is Apple Silicon or Intel?

The chip type determines which file to download.

1. Click the **Apple** menu in the top-left corner.
2. Choose **About This Mac**.
3. Look for the **Chip** or **Processor** line.
4. If it shows **Apple M1**, **M2**, **M3**, **M4** or another Apple chip, choose **ARM64**.
5. If it shows **Intel**, choose **X64**.

If in doubt, do not download both files. Check the **About This Mac** screen again before continuing.

## Where do I download the correct file?

The official files are published as DMGs in the PIANO LouvorJA releases for macOS.

1. Open [download](/download).
2. Choose **macOS**.
3. Select **DMG ARM64** for Apple Silicon or **DMG X64** for Intel.
4. Wait for the download to finish.
5. Open the **Downloads** folder.

Always download from the official page. Files with similar names sent by third parties may not be the right version.

## How do I install the DMG?

The DMG opens as a small window with the application and the Applications folder.

1. Double-click the downloaded DMG file.
2. Wait for the installation window to appear.
3. Drag **PIANO LouvorJA** into the **Applications** folder shown in the window.
4. Wait for the copy to finish.
5. Open **Finder**.
6. Go to **Applications**.
7. Double-click **PIANO LouvorJA**.
8. After confirming it opens, eject the installer disk from Finder.

Do not leave the application running from inside the DMG only. Copying it to **Applications** prevents it from depending on the downloaded file.

## macOS blocked the first launch. What should I do?

This can happen with applications downloaded from the internet. Open it once through the right-click menu.

1. Open **Finder** and go to **Applications**.
2. Locate **PIANO LouvorJA**.
3. Click the icon with the right mouse button or with Control held down.
4. Choose **Open**.
5. In the confirmation message, click **Open** again.
6. From the next time on, open it normally from Launchpad, Dock or Applications.

Do not change your Mac's security settings unless necessary. This procedure only allows the application you chose to open.

## What should I do on first launch?

PIANO prepares the catalog and keeps your information on the device itself.

1. Open PIANO LouvorJA from **Applications**.
2. Keep the internet connected during the first preparation.
3. Adjust the screen size with the **−** and **＋** buttons in the header, if needed.
4. Search for a hymn from the Adventist Hymnal in the **Media Center**.
5. Download the hymns and songbooks the team will use in the service.
6. Build the program in **Liturgy**.
7. Open **Settings** and adjust colors, background and projection size.

Downloads can continue later. If the app closes, the saved progress remains available when you come back.

## How do I test before the service?

A simple test avoids surprises during the praise or the message.

1. Open a hymn in the **Media Center**.
2. Click **Project**.
3. Move between verses and chorus.
4. Open the **Bible**, choose a verse and project it.
5. In **Utilities**, test the clock, timer or countdown.
6. In **Settings**, increase the font size if the lyrics look small.

To use a TV, projector or another screen, connect it beforehand and run the complete test with the team. PIANO separates the operation screen from the screen the congregation sees.

## How do I keep PIANO organized?

A prepared computer makes the Sabbath routine easier.

1. Keep the DMG only if you need to reinstall; the app runs from the **Applications** folder.
2. Download hymns, songbooks and liturgy content in advance.
3. Use categories in the liturgy, such as **Sabbath School**, **Praise**, **Offering** and **Message**.
4. Make a backup in **Settings** before switching computers.
5. Test the media files before the program starts.

## How do I organize the liturgy before the service?

The liturgy gathers the items of the service in an order the team can follow without searching for content during the program.

1. Open **Liturgy** and create a new program.
2. Create categories, such as **Welcome**, **Praise**, **Message** and **Closing**.
3. Add songs, passages, videos, images, PDFs or presentations.
4. Write notes for the team; they do not appear on the church screen.
5. Drag items to adjust the order.
6. Save and reopen to double-check.
7. During the service, advance item by item and track what has been completed.

## How do I prepare content for offline use?

What has already been downloaded in PIANO stays available on the Mac even if the connection drops.

1. Connect to the internet during the week.
2. Open the hymns that will be used.
3. Tap **download** on the needed items.
4. Wait for the progress bars to finish.
5. Also confirm the video, PDF and local presentation files of the liturgy.
6. Run a test without relying on a new search.
7. If you close the app, the download progress is preserved.

## How do I use the Stage on a TV?

Use the Stage when a TV connected to the network will be the congregation's screen.

1. Connect the TV or receiver to the church network.
2. Open the Stage on the device connected to the TV.
3. On the Mac, open the **Remote control and Stage** area.
4. Choose local or server pairing, according to what the screen shows.
5. Confirm the code or data displayed.
6. Project a hymn and advance one verse.
7. Confirm that the TV shows only the projection.

## Final checklist before the service

1. Confirm that PIANO opens on the chosen device.
2. Confirm that hymns and materials are available.
3. Confirm that the liturgy is in the correct order.
4. Confirm that the church screen receives a test projection.
5. Confirm text size and contrast against the hall's background.
6. Confirm network, power and the necessary cables.
7. Leave one person in charge of the operation.
